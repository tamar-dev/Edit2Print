# Privacy Policy — Edit2Print

**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

Edit2Print is a browser extension for editing and printing selected webpage content or an entire webpage.

## Data processed
When you choose Edit2Print from the browser's context menu, the extension copies the selected content or, if nothing is selected, the webpage body into an editable preview. This may include text, links, and image URLs present on that page. The extension does not read pages automatically in the background.

## Storage and transmission
- Content for the print preview is temporarily stored in Chrome's local extension storage and deleted after the preview loads it.
- Edit2Print does not operate a server to collect your content, and it does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- Images in the preview may load from their original websites, which can receive normal network requests.
- Printing and saving PDFs are handled by Chrome and your operating system.

## Permissions
- `contextMenus`: add the right-click command.
- `activeTab` and `scripting`: read the active page after you invoke the command.
- `storage`: transfer content temporarily to the preview tab.

## Limitations
Some protected browser pages and embedded content cannot be accessed. Complex website layouts may not be reproduced exactly.
