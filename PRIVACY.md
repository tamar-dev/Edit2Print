# Privacy Policy — Just Print Selection

Just Print Selection processes only the text and images that you choose to print from the Chrome context menu.

- It does not send your selected content to a developer-operated server.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or user accounts.
- The selected content is temporarily stored locally in Chrome extension storage so that the print preview can open in a new tab. The stored print job is deleted when the preview reads it.
- Images referenced by selected webpages may still load from their original websites in the print preview; those sites may receive normal image requests.
- The extension uses `contextMenus` for the right-click action, `activeTab` and `scripting` to read your explicitly selected content, and `storage` for the temporary print job.

The extension cannot promise that browser printing or saving a PDF leaves no local traces; those are managed by Chrome and your operating system.
